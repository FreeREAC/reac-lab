# captures

Packet captures (`.pcap` / `.pcapng`) of REAC traffic, with a short notes file per
capture describing the gear, the link, and what the capture shows. Decode them with
[reac-tools](https://github.com/FreeREAC) and against the wire format in
`reac-protocol`.

Captures are added as they are cleared for publication (no site-identifying frames).
